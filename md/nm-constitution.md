# Night Market Constitution ##
## How To Night Market ##

Night Market is a food rescue and free distribution network on a mission: to create radically inclusive local culture around food equity, sustainability, and resiliency, and to celebrate what we have in common: we all get hungry! Night Market is not a service for the poor or needy, it's an adventure for everyone. Our constitituion reflects our optimism that healthy food culture is the cornerstone for solving the many social ills that face us today. It provides a how-to guide for running a safe, fun, and effective market, ad for being a safe, fun, and effective marketeer.

A quick note on this document: it is a "living" document, designed to be replicated and edited by anyone with an idea on how to change or improve it. To ensure that it is maxmially usefuly, it is recommended to follow a simple pattern: make sure that each secondary heading (two hashtags like ## ) is followed by a short, tldr, summary paragraph. This way our Discord Bot, Crabapple, can share a brief summary of the document to new marketeers during the onboarding process.
